# Creative Task Coach — 3-Minute Demo Script

---

## 1. Introduce the Problem (~30 seconds)

> "We've all been there — you sit down to do creative work and you have a list of ten things to do, but you have no idea where to start. Should you work on the thing that's due soonest? The thing that's hardest? The thing that will make the biggest impact?
>
> Regular to-do lists don't answer that question. They just show you what needs to be done — not what to do *first* or *how* to begin.
>
> That's the problem we set out to solve."

---

## 2. Explain the Solution (~30 seconds)

> "We built the **Creative Task Coach** — an AI-powered web app that acts like a personal coach for creative professionals.
>
> You enter a task, and the app evaluates it across five dimensions: difficulty, estimated time, urgency, creative effort required, and overall importance. Then it gives you a suggested first step and a plain-language explanation of why that's the right place to start.
>
> It doesn't just organize your work — it helps you *begin*."

---

## 3. Walk Through the App (~45 seconds)

> "Let me show you how it works.
>
> Here on the home page, I'll enter a task. I'll type: *'Record a podcast episode about AI in education.'* I'll give it a due date of tomorrow, mark the importance as high, and hit **Evaluate Task**.
>
> The app sends that to our backend, which runs it through the AI and stores the result.
>
> And here's what comes back — a difficulty score of 4 out of 5, an estimated time of 90 minutes, urgency is High, creative effort is Deep Focus.
>
> And the suggested first step: *'Draft a 5-bullet outline of the key points you want to cover before you hit record.'*
>
> That's exactly the kind of advice a good creative coach would give you. Specific, actionable, and matched to the task."

---

## 4. Explain How AI Is Used (~30 seconds)

> "Under the hood, we're using the OpenAI API with a structured JSON schema response — so the AI doesn't just return free text, it fills out a defined structure every time: difficulty score, urgency level, creative effort category, suggested first step, and explanation.
>
> That structure is what lets us display the results cleanly and store them consistently in our SQLite database. The AI isn't just generating text — it's making a structured decision."

---

## 5. Explain How IBM Bob Helped (~30 seconds)

> "IBM Bob was a core part of how we built this. We used it throughout the project — not just for writing code, but as a real development partner.
>
> Bob helped us scaffold the Express backend and the OpenAI integration, design the JSON evaluation schema, debug errors in our API calls, and write this documentation.
>
> When something wasn't working, we'd describe the problem to Bob and it would walk us through exactly what was wrong and how to fix it. It saved us hours of digging through docs."

---

## 6. Real-World Value (~15 seconds)

> "Creative professionals — writers, designers, developers, musicians — lose real time every day just deciding where to start. This app gives that time back.
>
> It's a small nudge in the right direction, powered by AI, that helps people move from stuck to started.
>
> Thank you."

---
